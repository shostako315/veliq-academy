## Key points

- Born in 1760 near Honjo Warigesui in Edo (now around Kamezawa, Sumida, Tokyo); his childhood name was Tokitarō. In 1778 he entered the studio of the ukiyo-e artist Katsukawa Shunshō
- Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji (c. 1830; 46 sheets in all) includes Under the Wave off Kanagawa
- He changed his art name from Shunrō to Sōri, Hokusai, Iitsu and Gakyō Rōjin Manji, and died in 1849, aged 90 by Japanese count

Hokusai began as an ukiyo-e artist of the Katsukawa school and went on to make painting manuals, landscape prints and paintings. ==Each time he changed his name his style changed too, so his life can be divided into periods by those names.== He is said to have moved house 93 times.

## Life

| Year | Event |
|---|---|

| 1779 | Issues actor prints under the name Shunrō |
| 1814 | First volume of the Hokusai Manga |
| 1817 | Paints a giant Daruma on paper the size of 120 tatami mats at the Nishi Honganji branch temple in Nagoya |

| Last years | Visits Obuse in Shinano and paints the ceiling pictures Male Wave and Female Wave for a festival float |
| 10 May 1849 (Kaei 2, 4th month, 18th day) | Dies, aged 90 by Japanese count |

==The giant Daruma in Nagoya is said to have been an event to promote the Hokusai Manga amid the crowds of the city.== He spent almost all of his ninety years within what is now Sumida ward.

## Style

- Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji shows Fuji from many places, seasons and weathers
- In Under the Wave off Kanagawa the sky and water use Prussian blue (bero-ai, "Berlin indigo")
- The Hokusai Manga is a painting manual whose first volume appeared in 1814

Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji is a series of landscape prints showing Mount Fuji from various places, seasons and weathers. ==It has 46 sheets, more than the "thirty-six" of its title.==

In Under the Wave off Kanagawa a breaking wave looms over small boats, with Mount Fuji set behind. It is an ōban multicolour woodblock print published by Nishimuraya Yohachi; the sky and water use Prussian blue. The Art Institute of Chicago owns three impressions of this design, all later than the first printing.

Because prints were pulled many times from the same blocks, one design is held by many museums around the world. ==The Art Institute of Chicago's Under the Wave off Kanagawa is signed "Hokusai aratame Iitsu hitsu" (painted by Iitsu, formerly Hokusai).==

## Where to see his work

The Art Institute of Chicago holds Under the Wave off Kanagawa, Rainstorm Beneath the Summit, Senju in Musashi Province, Mitsui Shop at Suruga-chō in Edo, Fuji from the Minobu River and Kajikazawa in Kai Province; The Metropolitan Museum of Art holds Shimomeguro, Fujimigahara in Owari Province, Ejiri in Suruga Province and others. ==Different sheets of one series survive split between Chicago and New York.==

In Japan, the Tokyo National Museum holds 12 sheets of Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji. In Sumida, where Hokusai was born and raised, the Sumida Hokusai Museum opened in 2016 (2-7-2 Kamezawa, Sumida; 5 minutes' walk from Ryōgoku station on the Toei Ōedo line). In Obuse, Nagano Prefecture, which he visited late in life, the Hokusai-kan opened in 1976 and holds many of his paintings. The ceiling paintings Male Wave and Female Wave of the Kanmachi float and Dragon and Phoenix of the Higashimachi float belong to neighbourhood associations of Obuse and are on deposit at the Hokusai-kan. Male Wave is a late painting of breaking wave crests.

## FAQ

### What art names did Hokusai use?

==Shunrō, Sōri, Hokusai, Iitsu, Gakyō Rōjin Manji and others.== His name changes are counted as more than twenty, or more than thirty.

### How many sheets are in Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji?

Forty-six.

### How long did he live?

He died in 1849 (Kaei 2, 4th month, 18th day), aged 90 by Japanese count.

### When was his first work?

He entered Katsukawa Shunshō's studio in 1778 and issued actor prints under the name Shunrō the following year, 1779.

### What is the Hokusai Manga?

A painting manual whose first volume appeared in 1814.

### What was the giant Daruma in Nagoya?

A Daruma he painted in 1817 on paper the size of 120 tatami mats at the Nishi Honganji branch temple in Nagoya. It is said to have been an event to promote the Hokusai Manga.

### What remains in Obuse?

The ceiling paintings Male Wave and Female Wave of the Kanmachi festival float and Dragon and Phoenix of the Higashimachi float, owned by neighbourhood associations and on deposit at the Hokusai-kan.

### What kind of print is Under the Wave off Kanagawa?

One sheet of Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji, an ōban colour woodblock print of about 1830–33. The Art Institute of Chicago's impression is 25.4 × 37.6 cm, published by Nishimuraya Yohachi and signed "Hokusai aratame Iitsu hitsu" — a work from the period when he used the name Iitsu.
