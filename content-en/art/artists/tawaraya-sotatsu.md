## Key points

- Neither the year of his birth nor of his death is known. He appears to have run a painting shop called Tawaraya in Kyoto
- He painted gold and silver underpaintings for the calligraphy of Hon'ami Kōetsu, and by 1630 (Kan'ei 7) held the rank of hokkyō
- He is credited with creating tarashikomi, a technique that uses the spreading of ink and pigment

Tawaraya Sōtatsu was a painter active in Kyoto at the beginning of the Edo period. Wind God and Thunder God at Kennin-ji has no signature or seal, but is regarded as genuine and as the masterpiece of his late years. ==About a hundred years later Ogata Kōrin copied these screens and carried on Sōtatsu's style.==

## Life

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1602 (Keichō 7) | Restoration of the Heike Nōkyō sutras at Itsukushima Shrine; decorates covers and endpapers |
| Early 1600s – Genna era | Paints gold and silver underpaintings for poem cards and scrolls written by Hon'ami Kōetsu |
| 1621 | Pine sliding doors and cedar doors with fabulous beasts at Yōgen-in |
| 1630 (Kan'ei 7) | Holds the rank of hokkyō; paints three pairs of gold screens on the order of the retired Emperor Go-Mizunoo and copies the Saigyō Monogatari scroll |
| c. 1642 | Presumed to die (Hyakka Jiten Mypedia) |

## Style

- Areas of colour that use the spreading of tarashikomi
- Decorative pictures with gold grounds and gold and silver paint

The Nihon Daihyakka Zensho says Sōtatsu created tarashikomi and established a way of painting with areas of colour that exploit spreading. In the mid-Edo period Ogata Kōrin became deeply devoted to Sōtatsu's art and brought his style to completion.

The Tokyo National Museum explains that in Kōrin's copy the thunder god looks at the wind god, whereas in Sōtatsu's screens he looks down on the world below.

## Where to see his work

The original Wind God and Thunder God is sometimes shown in exhibitions at the Kyoto National Museum. Kennin-ji (Higashiyama, Kyoto) displays high-definition replica screens.

## FAQ

### When did he live?

His dates are unknown. Records of the 1602 restoration of the Heike Nōkyō and of his rank of hokkyō in 1630 show he was active in Kyoto in the early Edo period.

### What is tarashikomi?

A technique of dropping more ink or colour into ink or pigment before it dries, using the way it spreads. Sōtatsu is credited with creating it.

### How was he related to Ogata Kōrin?

Kōrin was deeply devoted to Sōtatsu's art and copied Wind God and Thunder God.
