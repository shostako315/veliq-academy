## Key points

- Painted in the village of Grez-sur-Loing near Paris, where Kuroda stayed with his friend, the painter Kume Keiichirō
- The model was Maria Billault, the daughter of the family Kuroda lodged with
- Shown at the 1891 Salon of the Société des Artistes Français as "Lecture en été" (Reading in Summer)

Reading is an oil painting of a young woman reading a book. The Kuroda Memorial Hall notes that a woman reading was a common subject at the time, and that the close framing on her absorbed face and the warm sunlight create an intimate mood. ==Kuroda had gone to France to study law, became a painter, and sent this picture to the Salon.==

## What it shows

A young woman reading a book. The model, Maria Billault, was the daughter of the village butcher with whom Kuroda lodged; he painted several major works of her in the village.

## How it was made

Kuroda went to France in 1884 to study law and studied painting under Raphaël Collin. After the Salon, Reading was sent to Japan and shown at the fourth Meiji Fine Arts Society exhibition in 1892. Kuroda returned to Japan the following year, 1893.

## What to look at in front of it

- The face of the woman, eyes lowered to the book
- The warm colour of the sunlight falling into the picture
- The close framing on the figure

## Where to see it

Held by the Tokyo National Museum and shown at certain times at the Kuroda Memorial Hall in Ueno. Check the websites of the Tokyo National Museum and the Kuroda Memorial Hall for viewing days.

## FAQ

### Where was it painted?

In the village of Grez-sur-Loing near Paris.

### Who was the model?

Maria Billault, the daughter of the village butcher, in whose house Kuroda lodged.

### What was its title at the Salon?

"Lecture en été" (Reading in Summer), at the 1891 Salon of the Société des Artistes Français.
