Irises — Ogata Kōrin's clusters of irises on gold, a National Treasure

Ogata Kōrin's Irises (Nezu Museum, National Treasure). A pair of six-panel screens from the 18th century with clusters of irises painted in high-quality pigments on gold. Why it is seen as the first peak of Kōrin, who began painting in his thirties, what to look at and where to see it.

Artist
Ogata Kōrin
Original title
燕子花図屏風（Kakitsubata-zu byōbu）
Date
Edo period, 18th century
Medium
Colour on gold-leafed paper
Dimensions
Pair of six-panel screens
Collection
Nezu Museum
Designation
National Treasure (designated 9 June 1951)

Key points

  • A pair of six-panel screens with clusters of irises in bloom on gold. A National Treasure, held by the Nezu Museum
  • The blue of the flowers and green of the leaves are laid on the gold in high-quality pigments
  • The Nezu Museum calls it the first artistic peak of Kōrin, who began painting in his thirties

Irises is a pair of folding screens on which Ogata Kōrin painted clusters of irises on gold. The Nezu Museum describes it as a work painted with generous use of high-quality pigments.

What it shows

Clusters of irises growing on a gold ground. The blue of the flowers and green of the leaves continue across both six-panel screens.

How it was made

Colour on paper laid with gold leaf. The Nezu Museum says it was painted with generous use of high-quality pigments and calls it the first artistic peak of Kōrin, who took up painting in his thirties. It dates from the Edo period, 18th century.

What to look at in front of it

  • The depth of the blue of the flowers, the thickness of colour laid in fine pigment
  • How the clusters are arranged and the intervals between them
  • The extent of the bare gold

Where to see it

Nezu Museum (Minami-Aoyama, Tokyo). It is shown only at certain times of year. Check the museum's website for the schedule.

FAQ

Where is it?

The Nezu Museum, Minami-Aoyama, Minato, Tokyo.

When was it designated a National Treasure?

On 9 June 1951 (Shōwa 26).

What does it show?

Clusters of irises in bloom on a gold ground.
